Little Explorers

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 6, 2026

A little world, with privacy in mind.

You can play without creating an account. Your discoveries and settings are saved on your device. Advertising and in-app purchases are disabled in the current Android testing release.

1. Who we are

Little Explorers is developed by Matheus Rotta Alves, using the name Little Worlds. In this policy, “we” means the developer. This policy covers Little Explorers and this privacy-policy website.

For privacy questions or support, contact matheusrotta7@gmail.com. The Android app package is com.littleworlds.littleexplorers.

2. Information used by the game

The game stores information locally so it can remember your progress and preferences, including:

  • Collected objects, achievements, discovered tools, and locally saved arcade scores.
  • Flight mode and control preferences, including pitch direction, touch-stick settings, and any saved audio preferences.
  • Your selected aircraft and locally saved aircraft availability or ownership state.

We do not operate player accounts, a developer-hosted cloud-save service, or a gameplay database. These saves are not sent to us by the game. The game does not provide chat, public profiles, or player content uploads.

We do not ask the game to access your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. We do not use developer-operated behavioral analytics or automatic crash-report collection in the current release. Technical logs may exist locally; if you choose to send a bug report, you decide which information to include.

3. Advertising and purchases

In the current Android testing release (version 1.0, build 9), advertising and in-app purchases are switched off. The game does not initialize its advertising or purchase flows in this configuration, display ads, or charge you for aircraft.

The project includes Unity advertising and purchasing components for possible future releases. Their presence does not mean these features are currently active. Before enabling ads, purchases, or materially different data collection, we will update this policy and provide the disclosures and choices required for that release, including appropriate protections for children.

We do not sell players’ personal information or use it for targeted advertising in the current release.

4. Children and families

Little Explorers is intended for a broad audience, including children. The current game does not ask children to submit a name, email address, date of birth, or other personal details, and does not include behavioral advertising or social messaging.

A parent or guardian can manage or remove local saves using the options described below. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate. Children should ask a parent or guardian before contacting support.

5. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever message, device details, screenshots, or other attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer questions, investigate issues, and handle privacy requests. Email is processed by our email provider, Google, as necessary to deliver and store the correspondence.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes, resolving disputes, security, or applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information. You can ask us to delete correspondence, subject to any lawful retention requirement.

6. Platforms, Unity, and this website

The game is built with Unity. Google Play, your operating system, and their services may separately process account, installation, device, diagnostic, or store information according to your platform settings and their own policies. For example, Google Play may make aggregate testing or stability information available to developers. We use any such information to support and improve the game, not to build advertising profiles.

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub receives technical request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to serve and secure the website. We have not added analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, external fonts, or contact forms to this page.

Relevant provider policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

These providers may process information in countries outside your own. Their policies explain their processing, retention, international-transfer safeguards, and privacy choices. A link to a provider policy does not mean all of that provider’s services are active in the game.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

Local progress remains on your device until you reset or remove it. The game’s reset option clears achievements and collectibles; it keeps control preferences, tool discoveries, and aircraft ownership or selection data. To remove all app-local data on Android, use your device’s app settings to clear Little Explorers’ storage. This also removes your local progress and settings.

Device or operating-system backups may retain or restore app data depending on your settings; manage those backups through your platform account. We do not operate a separate cloud backup of your game saves. There is no Little Explorers account to delete.

We cannot remotely view or erase saves held only on your device. For information you sent to support, contact the email address above.

8. Your rights and security

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a portable copy of personal information we hold about you, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights; we may ask for proportionate information to verify your request.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, we process support information for our legitimate interest in responding to requests and maintaining the game, to meet legal obligations where applicable, or on consent where required. We do not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about players.

We use reasonable safeguards for information under our control, but no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be brought to players’ attention through an appropriate in-game or release notice, with consent obtained where required.
